
# Wonder Shop

[Lien vers la page Itch.io](https://lenfos.itch.io/wonder-shop)

**Wonder Shop** is a **video game** created in October 2025 as part of UQAC's Autumn 2025 Wonder Jam. The theme of the Jam was ‘Counterfeit’ and three styles had to be respected by each team. My styles were:

 - Every man for himself.
 - Elementary, my dear Watson!
 - My friend? My friend is also my enemy.

As I was far from UQAC, I participated in this game jam alone, so I took on all the roles for a weekend.


## Gameplay and story

![WonderShop Game screen capure](/ProjectsFiles/WonderShopGameplay.jpg)

**Wonder Shop** is a game where you play the role of a pawn shop manager. Every day, customers will come in to try to sell their items. Be careful, though, because some of these items are fake. You will need to read the item description and date carefully to ensure that the item is genuine. However, if you are unsure, you can call in an expert to help you. Be careful not to abuse this service, as you can only call the expert three times per day.


The gameplay is simple: several customers arrive at your desk and show you their items. A more detailed menu for the item then opens, allowing you to see its details. It is then up to you to choose between buying, refusing, or calling an expert.


--- 
In this project, I worked as a developer and game designer. The biggest challenge was making all the gameplay decisions on my own without being able to consult with a team. The second challenge was completing the entire game within the given time frame. I therefore had to significantly reduce and adapt my scope so that the game could be completed by one person.

I participated in this jam remotely, wanting to have some fun working solo on this jam. My personal challenge was mainly to see if I was capable of producing a game on my own, which is also why the game is not eligible for competition in this jam.
